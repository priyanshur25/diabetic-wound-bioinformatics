Dataset: GSE199939

1. Dataset contains 61,343 rows and 25 columns.
2. There are 21 samples:
   - 10 diabetic wound samples
   - 11 non-diabetic control samples

3. No missing expression values were detected.
4. No duplicate gene identifiers were detected.
5. 21,387 genes showed zero expression across all samples.
6. Expression distributions showed a large number of
   low-expression genes and several high-expression outliers.
7. Median expression was zero across samples.
8. Mean TPM values were approximately 16.30 across samples.

Conclusion:
The expression data show a highly skewed distribution.
Log2(TPM + 1) transformation was applied for visualization.
Further analysis will investigate sample relationships
and identify differentially expressed genes.
