
WEEK 2: DIFFERENTIAL EXPRESSION ANALYSIS
Project: Diabetic Wound Bioinformatics
Dataset: GSE199939

1. OBJECTIVE
To identify genes showing differences in expression between diabetic wound samples and control samples.

2. DATASET AND PREPROCESSING
- Total genes in original dataset: 61,343
- Total samples: 21
- Diabetic wound samples: 10
- Control samples: 11
- Expression measure: TPM
- Filtering criterion: TPM > 1 in at least 3 samples
- Genes retained after filtering: 20,187
- Transformation: log2(TPM + 1)

3. DIFFERENTIAL EXPRESSION METHOD
- Statistical package: limma
- Linear model: lmFit()
- Group comparison: DiabeticWound - Control
- Empirical Bayes moderation: eBayes()
- Multiple testing correction: Benjamini-Hochberg (BH)

4. SIGNIFICANCE CRITERIA
- Adjusted p-value (FDR) < 0.05
- Absolute logFC > 1

Note: logFC represents differences on the log2(TPM + 1) expression scale.

5. RESULTS
- Total genes tested: 20,187
- Significant DEGs: 3,880
- Upregulated genes: 1,326
- Downregulated genes: 2,554

6. EXAMPLES OF SIGNIFICANT GENES
- SPATA20P1: logFC = 2.513
- MYBL2: logFC = 3.462
- GSTM2: logFC = -2.580
- ABCA10: logFC = -3.688
- VIPR1: logFC = -3.692
- AURKA: logFC = 2.450
- PPL: logFC = -4.871

7. OUTPUT FILES
- all_differential_expression_results.csv
- significant_DEGs.csv
- upregulated_genes.csv
- downregulated_genes.csv

Location: results/tables/

8. LIMITATIONS
- The analysis uses TPM expression values rather than raw RNA-seq counts.
- limma was applied to log2-transformed TPM values.
- Results should be treated as exploratory.
- Potential batch effects and other confounding factors have not yet been assessed.
- Biological interpretation and pathway-level analysis remain to be performed.
