WEEK 4: VISUALIZATION AND INTERPRETATION
Project: Diabetic Wound Bioinformatics
Dataset: GSE199939

1. OBJECTIVE
To visualize gene expression patterns, differential expression results, and functional enrichment findings.

2. VISUALIZATIONS GENERATED

A. Expression Distribution
- Plot: expression_distribution.png
- Displays the distribution of gene expression across samples.
- Log2(TPM + 1) was used for visualization.

B. Principal Component Analysis (PCA)
- Plot: PCA_diabetic_wound.png
- PCA was performed using genes with TPM > 1 in at least 3 samples.
- PC1 explained 40.95% of variance.
- PC2 explained 17.26% of variance.
- Together, PC1 and PC2 explained 58.21% of variance.
- Diabetic wound and control samples showed separation along PC1.
- PCA is exploratory and does not establish statistical significance.

C. Volcano Plot
- Plot: volcano_plot.png
- Displays log fold change against statistical significance.
- Thresholds: adjusted p-value < 0.05 and absolute logFC > 1.
- 1,326 upregulated and 2,554 downregulated genes were identified.

D. Heatmap of Top 50 Differentially Expressed Genes
- Plot: heatmap_top50_DEGs.png
- Displays expression patterns of the 50 genes with the smallest adjusted p-values.
- Expression values were log2(TPM + 1) transformed and row-scaled.
- The heatmap showed distinct clustering of control and diabetic wound samples.
- Row scaling represents relative expression for each gene.

E. GO Biological Process Enrichment
- Plots: GO_BP_dotplot.png, GO_BP_upregulated.png, GO_BP_downregulated.png
- Highlights enriched biological processes associated with DEGs.
- Major themes include inflammation, extracellular matrix organization, angiogenesis, and epidermal differentiation.

F. KEGG Pathway Enrichment
- Plots: KEGG_dotplot.png, KEGG_upregulated.png, KEGG_downregulated.png
- Displays pathways enriched among differentially expressed genes.
- Relevant findings include integrin signalling, ECM-receptor interaction, cytokine signalling, IL-17 signalling, and cornified envelope formation.

4. LIMITATIONS
- PCA and heatmap patterns are exploratory visualizations.
- Sample clustering does not independently establish biological significance.
- Potential batch effects and confounding factors have not been fully assessed.
- Enrichment visualizations represent over-representation, not direct pathway activity.

5. WEEK 4 CONCLUSION
The visualizations provide complementary views of expression distributions, sample-level variation, differentially expressed genes, and enriched biological processes and pathways.
Together, they support the interpretation of altered inflammatory, vascular, extracellular matrix, and epidermal-associated gene-expression patterns in diabetic wound samples.
